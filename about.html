About IIVET Scope Center

A locally-run, franchise-authorised study centre carrying forward IIVET's national skill-training standards.

–– Our Story

Bringing a national curriculum to a local classroom.

IIVET Scrop France operates as an authorised franchise study centre of the Indian Institute of Vocational Education & Training (IIVET) — a body that designs standardised diploma and certificate programmes across Information Technology, vocational trades, teacher training and management.

Rather than build a curriculum from scratch, our centre plugs directly into IIVET's syllabus, exam calendar and digital certificate-verification system, while keeping the classroom experience, batch sizes and mentoring local to Kanpur.

Every learner who joins us studies the same modules, sits the same DOCP exams, and receives a certificate that carries the same recognition as any other IIVET partner centre in the network.

Our Mission

Make industry-relevant, hands-on training accessible close to home.

Our Vision

Be the most trusted skill-training centre in the region.

Our Promise

Small batches, real practicals, and honest placement support.

Our Network

Part of IIVET's franchise network of study centres across India.

–– A Note From Our Director

"Skill training only matters if it leads somewhere."

When we took on the IIVET franchise for this region, our one condition to ourselves was simple: no course goes on the prospectus unless it leads to a real job, a real business, or a real next step for the student. That's the filter every batch here is run through.

We're proud to be part of a network that treats certification seriously — digital verification, a fixed exam calendar, and a syllabus that's reviewed against what industry actually needs.
